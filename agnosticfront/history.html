Band History

Early Years
Agnostic Front formed in New York City in the early 1980s and became part of the New York hardcore scene.
Victim in Pain
The band's first full-length album, Victim in Pain, was released in 1984.
Cause for Alarm
Cause for Alarm was released in 1986 and included more metal influence in the band's hardcore sound.
Legacy
Agnostic Front became one of the well-known bands connected with New York hardcore and continued releasing music for many years.
